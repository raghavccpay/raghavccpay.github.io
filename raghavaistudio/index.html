On this device

Edit the picture you already have.

Grades, text, logos, object fills, and social sizes. The upload is kept as the original. Generative edits stay off until you explicitly send a reduced copy.

Original stays put.

Crops, fills, and exports make a new file. Undo still reaches the picture you opened.

Grades, not filters-as-theater

Brightness, color, sharpness, and film looks change pixels on device. Styles are color grades with an intensity control, not a generative restyle.

Words you can actually edit

New text is a layer. Flattened words can be detected or boxed, covered with nearby color, and replaced. The original typeface is not recovered.

Logos and marks

Upload a mark or place a generic icon. Move, scale, recolor, and restack it. Trademark logos are not in the icon set.

Honest removal

Brush, rectangle, or color select. The gap is filled from neighboring pixels. It is not a segmentation model, and fine texture can stay soft.

Social frames

Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Telegram, Pinterest, Snapchat. Cover or pad. Never stretch.

Export with a floor

Compare before and after. Resize without silent upscaling. If a file-size target would crush quality under about 45%, the optimizer says so.

Provenance already in a file is shown, not stripped and not forged. A re-encode is a new file. The project history lives in the browser. No account is required.
